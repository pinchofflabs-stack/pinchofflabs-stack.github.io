Potty Log — Privacy Policy

Effective: 2026-09-18 (v1.3)

Sensitive Data Notice (Korea PIPA Article 23)

This app processes bowel and urine records, Bristol stool scale types and symptom information related to gut health. This is sensitive information under Article 23 of Korea’s Personal Information Protection Act, and it is processed only after separate, explicit consent given when the app is first opened.

· What is collected: bowel and urine records (time and duration), Bristol type, symptom tags, notes, hydration records
· Purpose: saving and viewing records, showing statistics, analyzing health patterns (all processed on the device)
· Retention: for the retention period you set (365 days by default), then deleted automatically
· Sharing with third parties: none
· Right to decline: if you decline, saving records is limited. You can withdraw consent in Settings at any time.

Information We Collect

All bathroom activity records are stored only on your device by default. No account is required and no identifying information such as a name or email is requested.

Ads & Purchases

Free users are shown Google AdMob ads. Ad requests are always non-personalized. Payments are processed by Apple, and RevenueCat (with an anonymous ID) is used to confirm purchases. Pro users see no ads.

No Sale of Health Data (Nevada SB 370 / Washington MHMDA)

Potty Log never sells your health data to third parties and never uses it for ad targeting. Ads are never linked to your records.

Retention

Records are kept only on your device for the retention period you set (30 days, 1 year or forever). You can delete old records or all data at any time in Settings → Data, and deleting the app deletes all data.

Your Rights

· Access and correction: you can view and edit every record in the app.
· Export: you can export your records as CSV or text.
· Deletion: you can delete single records or everything immediately. Because records are stored only on the device, deletion is immediate and complete.
· Withdrawing consent: you can withdraw consent to sensitive-data processing in Settings.
· Washington MHMDA: you have the right to consent to or decline the collection of health data, to request deletion, and to restrict processing.

Security

Records are stored inside the iOS app sandbox and are protected by your device lock. Records are not sent to any server, and no user account or record is stored on a server.

Children

This app is not intended for children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from children.

Contact

Privacy questions and deletion requests: haeker0907@gmail.com
(Requests are handled without delay and within 45 days at the latest. On-device deletion is immediate.)
