Atompia Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 6, 2026

Atompia is a chemistry game. We built it to work without collecting anything about you.

What stays on your device

Your progress (the stops you have cleared, your stars and when you earned them this week, your quiz review cards, your Lab discoveries and what your atoms are wearing) and your settings (music, sound effects, voice and haptics) are stored only on your device. We have no server and never receive them. Deleting the app deletes them.

The World map in My cards and in Learn more reads your device's region setting, on the device, to highlight your own country where the app has its figures. It is never stored or sent anywhere.

Game Center (optional)

If you are signed in to Game Center on your device, Atompia posts your star totals to two Game Center leaderboards ("All-time stars" and "Stars this week") and reports your progress toward in-game achievements. Each score is sent with a small number that says which element your atom is and which outfit and sky it has, so other players see your atom dressed that way next to your score in the Ranking tab; it says nothing else about you. To do this, the app uses your Game Center player identity and your scores, only to run the leaderboards and achievements. Game Center is run by Apple: your scores appear next to your Game Center nickname, where other players can see them, and as the developer we can see the top scores and nicknames on each leaderboard in App Store Connect (to remove cheating, for example). We don't store your Game Center identity ourselves and we don't combine it with any other data. Apple's handling of Game Center data is covered by Apple's privacy policy. If you are not signed in, nothing is posted. You can sign out at any time in Settings → Game Center.

In-app purchases

Every world in Atompia opens for free by playing. The optional purchases are "Unlock All Worlds", a shortcut that opens every world at once, and Style items (atom outfits, sky themes and sound packs). Purchases are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store; we never see your payment details. A grown-up question comes before every purchase. The app keeps only the list of products bought, on your device, so it can open what you own; it sends nothing to us. Family Sharing works for every purchase, and "Restore Purchases" brings them back on a new device.

No ads, no tracking, no analytics, no accounts

Atompia contains no advertising and no third-party analytics or tracking tools. It does not ask for your name, email, location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone.

Speech

Narration is audio included in the app, recorded ahead of time with the open-source Kokoro voice; any line without a recording is read on your device by Apple's built-in speech synthesizer. The app never records your voice, and nothing is sent anywhere.

Children's privacy

Atompia is rated 4+ and collects no personal information from anyone, including children. A Game Center account is managed by Apple and, for children, by a parent or guardian through Family Sharing and Screen Time, which can also turn off Game Center and in-app purchases.
