Atompia Support

Make molecules atom by atom.

How do new worlds open?

By playing. The first world is open from the start. Finish every stop in a world (one star is enough) and the next one opens. Every world opens this way, for free. A world that isn't open yet shows how many stops are left in the one before it.

What does "Unlock All Worlds" do?

It's an optional shortcut. One purchase opens every world right away, for anyone who doesn't want to wait. Everything in it also opens by playing. It's yours to keep and works across your family with Family Sharing. There are no ads and no subscriptions.

How do I restore a purchase on a new device?

Open the shortcut sheet by tapping a world that isn't open yet on the map, or go to My cards → Style. Tap "Restore Purchases". Use the same Apple Account you bought with.

Why does it ask a question before buying?

Every purchase starts with a grown-up check: a multiplication question or a number written in words, with three tries. It keeps purchases with grown-ups. Restore Purchases doesn't need it. To turn purchases off completely, use Screen Time on the device.

How do I change the sound?

Tap the gear on the map. You can turn Music, Sound effects, Voice (reading explanations aloud) and Haptics on or off. The silent switch on your device also silences the game. Sound packs in My cards → Style change how everything sounds.

How do I use Game Center?

Sign in under Settings → Game Center on your device, then open the Ranking tab to see the All-time and This week leaderboards. The Achievements button there shows your achievements. Game Center is optional: the game and your stars work the same without it.

Can other players see my atom's outfit?

Yes. In My cards, tap My atom. Pick any element you've met with Change element, and tap Dress up to give it an outfit and a sky. When you're signed in to Game Center, the Ranking tab shows every player with their own atom, dressed, next to their stars. When you change your look, the board shows it the next time you earn a star.

Where do the World map figures come from?

From published statistics, most of them official, such as the U.S. Geological Survey, the U.S. Department of Agriculture and Korea's Ministry of Trade, Industry and Energy. Each figure is shown with its year and source. Coastlines come from Natural Earth. The map shows no country borders. Things that are found everywhere, made by living things or made in factories say so in words instead of a map.
